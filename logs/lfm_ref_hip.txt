<|startoftext|>The history of the Roman Empire can be summarized as follows: The Roman Republic, founded in 509 BC, expanded through the Punic Wars, defeating Carthage and establishing dominance over the Mediterranean. This led to internal strife, culminating in the rise of Julius Caesar, who crossed the Rubicon in 49 BC, sparking a civil war. After Caesar's assassination in 44 BC, the Second Triumvirate took power, but internal conflicts continued. The Battle of Actium in 31 BC saw Octavian defeat Mark Antony and Cleopatra, establishing his supremacy. In 27 BC, the Senate granted him the title Augustus, marking the beginning of the Roman Empire
