The history of the Roman Empire can be summarized as follows:

1. **Founding of the Roman Republic (753 BC)**: Rome was founded as a city-state by the legendary twins Romulus and Remus. The city-state was initially ruled by kings, but in 509 BC, the Roman Republic was established after the last king, Tarquinius Superbus, was overthrown.

2. **Roman Republic (509 BC – 27 BC)**: The Roman Republic was a period of political and military expansion. Rome grew from a small city-state into a powerful empire
