<|startoftext|>The history of the Roman Empire can be summarized as follows: The Roman Republic, founded in 509 BC, expanded through military conquests across the Mediterranean, eventually leading to the rise of Julius Caesar, who crossed the Rubicon in 49 BC, triggering a civil war. After his assassination in 44 BC, the Second Triumvirate (Octavian, Mark Antony, and Lepidus) took control, but internal conflicts led to the Battle of Actium in 31 BC, where Octavian emerged victorious. In 27 BC, the Senate granted Octavian the title "Augustus," marking the beginning of the Roman Empire. Under Augustus, the empire entered a period of stability
