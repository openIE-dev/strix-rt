<|startoftext|>The history of the Roman Empire can be summarized as follows: The empire began with the rise of Augustus after the fall of the Roman Republic in 27 BCE. Under his rule and that of his successors, the empire expanded significantly, reaching its greatest territorial extent during the reign of Trajan (98–117 CE). The empire was governed by an emperor, with power often concentrated in the hands of a single ruler, though sometimes shared among a council. The Roman legal system, including the concept of citizenship, played a crucial role in administration.
